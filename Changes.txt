1.0.3
- 修复：与 Vehicle Framework／SmashTools 共存时，归档地图恢复因旧缓存或重复初始化而报地图 ID 冲突；补齐缓存释放和半初始化绘图区的失败清理。用户已确认本轮实测无明显问题。
  Fixed: archived maps could fail to restore with Vehicle Framework/SmashTools because of stale caches or duplicate initialization. Added cache cleanup and safe disposal of partially initialized map rendering resources. The user confirmed no apparent issues in this round of in-game testing.
- 新增：区域 prefab 保存并恢复室内温度与真空度，支持旋转；局部导入按格数与目标房间现有环境混合，露天和破口沿用原版规则。
  Added: region prefabs preserve indoor temperature and vacuum through rotation and reconstruction. Partial imports mix with the destination room by cell count; outdoor and breached rooms follow vanilla rules.
- 验证：两版构建与环境专项离线回归通过，Delta-V 教程已改为读取 prefab 环境设置，用户实测通过。
  Validation: builds for both supported versions and offline environment regression checks passed. Delta-V tutorials now read environment settings from prefabs, with in-game testing confirmed by the user.

1.0.2
- 修复：整图归档时可能出现压缩器缺失报错；恢复时使用归档自身的版本信息，避免受此前加载文件影响。
  Fixed: full-map archiving could report a missing compressor. Restoration now uses the archive's own version information instead of metadata left by a previously loaded file.
- 修复：整图恢复时可能丢失建筑风格来源、角色政策、艺术品故事及世界容器内容的引用。
  Fixed: full-map restoration could lose references to building style sources, pawn policies, art tales, and contents held by world objects.
- 修复：补齐地图恢复收尾和失败清理，避免存读档重入或地图生成期间启动归档操作。
  Fixed: completed map restoration finalization and failure cleanup, and prevented archive operations from overlapping save/load operations or map generation.

1.0.1
- 修复：与 Vehicle Map Framework 同时使用时，地面角色向载具内部目标射击可能会立即取消；现在可以正常开火
  Fixed: with Vehicle Map Framework installed, a pawn on the ground could have their shot at a target inside a vehicle cancelled immediately. They can now fire normally.
